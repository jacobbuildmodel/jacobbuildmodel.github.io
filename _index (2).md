title: "Weekly Brief"
description: "Regime, flows, forward catalysts, and disconfirmation — published weekly."
---

Each brief covers what kind of market this is and what changed, where money moved in dollar terms,
what dated events are coming in the next ninety days, a small number of research items outside the
mega-cap complex, a fixed twelve-name sector read, and a grading of live theses against their
falsifiers.

Figures carry [source tiers](/process/#source-tiering). Claims carry falsifiers. Nothing here is a
recommendation — see the [disclaimer](/disclaimer/).
