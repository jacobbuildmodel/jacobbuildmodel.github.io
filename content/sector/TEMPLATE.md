## The rotation

One paragraph. Which factor got paid this week and what changed. Name it — growth, value,
duration, quality, defensives, cyclicals, momentum.

## What moved

Prose, not raw tables. What's hot, what's accelerating (outperforming *and* by more than last
month — that's the signal, not just "outperforming"), what's cold, what's breaking down, and
where volume surged above 1.5x its 3-month average.

Every move paired with participation. A theme up on 0.6x volume is a weak signal and saying so
is the value, not the move itself.

## Where the sector contradicts itself

The most important section. Same-sector names telling opposite stories is the finding — not
sector agreement. If two names in one sector are at cycle-high margins while a third is coming
off a trough, "this sector" is not one trade. Say so plainly.
