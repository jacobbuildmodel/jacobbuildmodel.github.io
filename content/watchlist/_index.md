This is not a buy list. It is a record of what I'm researching and why.

Each entry carries four things: the **mechanism** — why capital would move, not that it moved; the
**question** I'm actually trying to answer; the **falsifier** — the observation that would kill the
idea; and a **confidence tier** (verified, inferred, or speculative).

Entries are added and retired as evidence arrives. A retired entry stays visible with the reason it
was retired, because the discards are the more instructive half of the record.

Nothing here is a recommendation — see the [disclaimer](/disclaimer/).
