## Baidu (BIDU) — Revenue miss dressed as a blowout

**What the headline says.** A stock-agnostic earnings-calendar feed shows Baidu printing EPS of 7.22 against a consensus estimate of 1.51, and revenue of $31.33B against an estimate of $4.68B — read at face value, a 378% EPS beat and a 570% revenue beat. That is the number a fast automated write-up, or a skim of the raw data, would run with today.

**What the filing says.** It isn't a filing yet, and that's the first finding: no 6-K has posted to SEC EDGAR for Baidu since one dated 2026-08-11 (a Form 4, unrelated). The filing-lag check fires cleanly here — the quarter isn't in structured filed data at all; everything below comes from Baidu's own unaudited press release and earnings call, cross-checked against two independent wire reproductions of it.

Once you correct for that, the "beat" isn't one. The vendor feed's 7.22 and 31.33B are Baidu's own RMB-denominated non-GAAP EPS and RMB revenue figures, compared against USD consensus estimates — a straight currency-unit collision, not a beat. On a like-for-like USD basis: revenue $4.62B, below the ~$4.74B analysts had estimated, down 4% YoY and 2% QoQ. Non-GAAP EPS $1.06, below estimates in the $1.39–1.51 range depending on which consensus source is used. GAAP EPS $0.85, GAAP net income $342M.

Two of the other standard checks ran on this print and found nothing, which is itself worth stating: net income ($342M) sits below GAAP operating income (~$446M) — no non-operating gain is inflating the headline the way it did on AMZN or GRAB recently. And the non-GAAP-vs-GAAP gap (non-GAAP net income $379M vs GAAP $342M) is about 0.8% of revenue — nowhere near the ~20%-of-revenue pattern that flagged CRWD, AXON and TEM. This isn't a name dressing up its economics; it's a data vendor mixing currencies.

**Why it matters.** Underneath the shrinking headline is one segment moving the other direction: AI Cloud Infra revenue was RMB7.3B, +50% YoY, and inside that, GPU Cloud specifically grew +283% YoY — accelerating from +184% the prior quarter. That's a second-derivative signal, not a level: the growth rate of the AI-infrastructure sliver is itself speeding up while the legacy search/ads-driven core it sits inside keeps shrinking. It's also a cross-border data point for anyone tracking AI-infrastructure capex mainly through US hyperscaler prints (NVDA/CRWV/NBIS/SMCI-type commitments) — China-side GPU cloud demand is independently accelerating on its own reporting cycle, which is corroboration, not the same mechanism.

**Provenance.** COMPANY — an unaudited earnings release and call, not yet a structured SEC filing. Baidu files 20-F/6-K as a foreign private issuer, so even once the 6-K posts, no US-GAAP XBRL structured data will ever exist for this name the way it does for a domestic 10-Q filer (the same limitation that hits BABA, ASML, SAP, GRAB, TSEM). Every figure above is COMPANY tier at best, permanently.

**Falsifier.** GPU Cloud YoY growth stays above ~250% next quarter → the acceleration is real and continuing. A drop back toward or below the prior quarter's 184% pace → this quarter's 283% was a pull-forward, not a trend. Resolves when Baidu reports Q3 2026; exact date not yet announced.

**Gaps.** No filed 6-K to check the press release against. Exact next-report date unknown. Analyst coverage: 18 price-target updates in the trailing year, but only 2 in the quarter immediately before this print — pre-print target-setting was thin even though the name is broadly covered historically (67 targets all-time, average $175.57). I did not pull a full segment breakdown beyond AI Cloud Infra (RMB7.3B) / AI Applications (RMB2.5B) / Baidu Core (RMB12.5B) / iQIYI (RMB6.3B) — deeper unit economics within AI Cloud aren't disclosed at this level of detail in the release.

**Depth: FIRST-PASS.** Anchored on an unaudited release, not a filed statement — treat as directional, not final, until the 6-K posts.
