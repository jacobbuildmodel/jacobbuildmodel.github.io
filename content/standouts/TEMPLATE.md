## [Company] (TICKER) — [the finding in six words or fewer]

**Mechanism.** Why capital would move. Not that it moved — the cause.

**What the filings say.** The period-pinned GAAP table, with the `period_end` stated for every
row. This is usually the single most valuable thing in the entry — the margin *series* is what
turns a headline multiple into a peak-quarter artefact, and no free screener shows the series.

**What the market is assuming.** The reverse-DCF framed as a testable claim: "this price
requires roughly X% growth for N years, which sits [above/within/below] the historical
achievement table." Never as a price target. Never as a valuation verdict.

**Depth.** Which stages actually ran and which didn't. "Stages 0–3 and 5 computed, Stage 4 not
run — no scenario tree" is exactly the right level of honesty. A reader calibrating how much to
trust this needs the depth tag, not just the conclusion.

**Falsifier.** Named KPI, threshold, date.

**Gaps.** What couldn't be verified and why — a foreign filer with no `us-gaap` concepts, a
sparse XBRL tag, a beta that couldn't be measured on a short listing. Name which.
