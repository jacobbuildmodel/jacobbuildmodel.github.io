The third layer. [News](/news/) said what happened. [Sector](/sector/) said which sectors the data
favours. This is which specific companies stand out within them — and, just as importantly, what
would prove that wrong.

Each entry carries four things: the **mechanism** — why capital would move, not that it moved; **why
it's outstanding in its sector** right now; a **falsifier** — the exact observation that would kill
the idea, and by when; and a **confidence tier** (verified, inferred, or speculative).

**This is analysis, not a recommendation, and it is not tailored to any individual reader's
circumstances.** The same page is shown to everyone regardless of their goals, holdings, or risk
tolerance — which is exactly what keeps it analysis instead of advice. If you're weighing an actual
decision, talk to a licensed financial adviser who can account for your specific situation. See the
[disclaimer](/disclaimer/) and the [Method](/process/) page for how entries are sourced and graded.

Entries are added and retired as evidence arrives. A retired entry stays visible with the reason it
was retired, because the discards are the more instructive half of the record.
