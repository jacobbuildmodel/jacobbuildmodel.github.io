Here you'll find the latest earnings reports, explained.

Companies report every quarter. The headline number gets copied across a dozen sites within
minutes, usually without anyone checking what sits behind it. That's the gap this section fills.

Each piece covers one company: what they reported, what it means for the business, what management
said about the months ahead, and which parts of the market it makes worth watching.

Written for someone with no finance background who wants to actually understand the numbers rather
than be told what to do with them.
