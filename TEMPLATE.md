> **The hook.** One or two sentences in plain English, no jargon, before any structure. The brief
> below is written densely; this is the doorway. Example shape: *"Money left energy for the third
> straight week while the price held — here's what that gap usually means, and the one print that
> would settle it."*

## Regime

What kind of market is this, and what changed this week. Name the factor being rewarded — growth,
cash flow, duration, defensives, cyclicals, quality, momentum. Rates, dollar, oil, credit spreads and
volatility appear only where they changed the regime. One paragraph. No data dump.

## Flows

Money movement measured in currency, not inferred from price. ETF creations and redemptions by sector
and theme, fund flows, notable 13F changes, insider-buying clusters.

Every figure needs a source and a date. Where flow and price disagree in direction, say so explicitly
and lead with the flow.

If no hard flow data was obtained this week, write exactly that and move on. Do not substitute price
action and call it flow.
