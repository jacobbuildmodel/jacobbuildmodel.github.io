I'm a student in Singapore studying markets, with a particular interest in where measured capital
flows disagree with price, and in the parts of the market that thin analyst coverage leaves
under-examined.

This site is the public half of a research process I've been building and revising. Each week I
publish an analysis of the market's regime, where money actually moved in dollar terms, what dated
events are coming that expectations haven't absorbed, and a small number of companies or themes I'm
researching — with the question I'm trying to answer about each and the evidence that would change
my mind.

It is deliberately not a stock-picking site. There are no recommendations, no positions, and no
performance claims. What's on offer is the reasoning: how a view gets built, what counts as evidence,
and what would count as being wrong. The rules I write under are documented in full on the
[Method](/process/) page.

I'm aiming at economics study and research work in investment management. If you're reading this in
that capacity — I'd rather be judged on the archive than on a CV line, which is roughly the point of
publishing it.
