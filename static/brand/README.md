# The mark

Jacob's design (23 November 2026): the void-deck beam, with scales weighing a gold feather
against a deep blue heart, on the red ground line. Drawn as SVG by hand-written code; no AI image.

- `S32.svg`: the reduced mark on a 32 px grid, as Jacob picked it.
- `S16.svg`: the 16 px mark on whole pixels, as he picked it; `static/favicon.svg` is a copy.
- `L1.svg`: the lockup, the mark beside the site's name.
- `../logo.svg`: S32's shapes for the masthead band (layouts/partials/header.html inlines it). The band
  is navy in both themes, so this copy takes the band's text colour and the lighter gold, blue and red
  that S32 uses in dark mode; a navy heart on a navy band would not show.
- The PNG and ICO icons in `static/` are S16 and S32 rendered by `tools/make_icons.py`.
