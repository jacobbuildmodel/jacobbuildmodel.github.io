# CP0 note 5: The beverage container deposit scheme

Input for the purpose review of 6 Nov 2026 (idea scout candidate 5). Not a verdict; no sealed
wording, no confidences.

**Stem.** Since 1 April 2026 drinks in plastic and metal containers of 150 ml to 3 litres carry a
10-cent refundable deposit, redeemed at reverse vending machines; from 1 October 2026 every
regulated drink must carry the deposit mark (NEA). The targets are at least 60 per cent returned in
the first year and 80 per cent from 2029 (CNA, 21 Sep 2026). About 1,070 machines opened at launch,
and return points are to double to around 2,000 within the first year (CNA, 6 Aug 2026; NEA).

**(a)** Explain how a deposit-refund system corrects the negative externality of unrecycled
containers, and why the return rate depends on the deposit's value against the consumer's time
cost of returning.

**(b)** Discuss whether raising the deposit or adding return points is more effective to reach the
80 per cent target at the lowest cost.

**Decision.** NEA and BCRS Ltd after year one, which ends 31 Mar 2027 (BCRS Ltd reports to NEA
within three months), on deposit level, return points and scope. NEA: "These insights will help us
improve the scheme" (ST Forum, 9 Oct 2026). A miss: raise the deposit, as Oregon did, or add
points, whichever costs less per extra container. A hit: the design stands. Stakes Low-Medium.

**Rivals.**
- Mr Raymond Lim (ST Forum, 30 Sep 2026, as cited by the scout) asks "whether the scheme has resulted in a meaningful
  increase in recycling rates"; Mr Cheong Wing Kiat (ST Forum, 31 Jul 2026, as cited by the
  scout): it "may be more trouble than it's worth".
- NEA and BCRS Ltd (ST Forum, 9 Oct 2026): "The early results are encouraging", with no rate.
- A sustainability writer (Substack, 17 Jul 2026): 80 per cent by year three is "ambitious", and
  return-point density is the key operational question.
- Oregon raised its deposit from 5 to 10 cents on 1 April 2017 after two years below 80 per cent
  (Oregon Legislature, HB 2746 staff summary, 2017).
- Already stated? Across schemes, yes: higher deposits go with higher return rates (Reloop, Global
  Deposit Book 2024, as cited by the scout), and Oregon's later rates are public. For Singapore,
  only interim container counts.

**Data (labels and coverage only).** BCRS Ltd's year-one report, due about 30 Jun 2027;
publication of units returned and units placed on the market is unconfirmed. SingStat M891371,
waste generated, recycled and disposed and recycling rate by type, annual, 2000-2025 (Plastic,
Ferrous Metal, Non-Ferrous Metal; a fallback only, as 2026 holds nine months of the scheme).
Return points by date: NEA and BCRS Ltd releases. Reference class: Reloop's book and dashboard,
operators' reports, Oregon's redemption reports. Time cost: MOM median income per hour (labels
unchecked).

**Method.** (a) Score the year-one rate against 60 per cent and against a sealed prior: a
cross-scheme regression of return rate on the deposit relative to the local median hourly wage and
on return points per 10,000 people. An LLM extracts these rows from scheme reports in several
languages; each is hand-checked against its source table. The seal fixes how the six-month
transition, when unmarked stock was still sold, enters the rate. (b) The same regression prices a
doubled deposit against more return points, with BCRS Ltd's cost per machine if published.

**Smallest effect that matters.** 5 percentage points of return rate per doubling of the deposit
relative to the hourly wage. The year-one score is a hit or a miss against 60 per cent.

**Positive control (coverage).** Units placed on the market and units returned must both be
published for April 2026 to March 2027; without the denominator there is no rate.

**G3 grid.** Yes = the 10-cent deposit misses its 60 per cent first-year target. Prior in per
cent, not a confidence.

| | Data says yes | Data says no |
|---|---|---|
| People believe it | "Ten cents was too little: the deposit missed its target" (30) | "A 10-cent deposit hit its first-year target" (30) |
| Nobody suspects it | hidden link (20) | no news (20) |

**Seal-by and kill.** Seal by 31 Mar 2027, the last day of year one. Kill: no commitment by then
from NEA or BCRS Ltd to publish the year-one rate with its denominator.

**Cost.** 4-5 working days.
