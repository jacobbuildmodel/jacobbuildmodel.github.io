## Ranking, by my reading of G0-G5

1. **CDC vouchers and hawker food prices**: a parliamentary question answered on annual data, a
   Budget 2027 choice between vouchers and cash, both parts testable with public tables, 4-5 days.
2. **Older workers and job opportunities for the young**: the sharpest decision (Re-Employment
   Credit and Flexi-Load Programme designed in H1 2027), but three annual steps give low power and
   (b) rests on evidence from abroad.
3. **Generative AI and entry-level graduate jobs**: the highest stakes and the PM's stated worry,
   held back by the exposure map, partial answers already public, and the highest cost (9-11 days).
4. **The RTS Link and heartland retail sales**: a hard news peg and named forecasts, but national
   data cannot see the Woodlands fear and (b) has little data behind it.
5. **The beverage container deposit scheme**: cheap, with a clear decision after year one, but
   low stakes and a stop if the year-one rate is never published.
